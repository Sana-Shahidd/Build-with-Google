# Golden Minute

**Every responder ready in 60 seconds — for people who can't read, type or speak English.**
Golden Minute is a multi-agent emergency coordination system. A bystander presses one
button, speaks in their own language, or scans a QR sticker on a helmet. Nine AI agents
(Intake, Verification, Triage, Dispatch, Hospital Prep, Debrief, Landmark, Voice Coach,
Preparedness) understand the report, merge duplicates, describe the place with local
landmarks, alert police/ambulance/hospitals, talk the bystander through first aid, and help
people prepare an Emergency Card or Place Plan in advance. Agents propose; deterministic
guardrails decide; humans stay in control.
